# Marketing strategy — Sept–Dec 2026 (summary)

*Built 11–13 Aug 2026. Client-facing plan lives at `armenian/plan/index.html` in the Cowork workspace (not in this repo).*

## Positioning
Manchester's only Armenian table. 58 years of fire, meze and wine — two minutes from Albert Square.
*(Original read "one family's fire" — corrected; three families have run it.)*

## Strategic spine
- The category is too small to win on search → win by being the answer to "where shall we go?" → paid social over search.
- Five audiences · six pillars (Fire, Table, Calendar, 58 Years, Cellar, Room).
- **December is sold in October.** UK festive bookings confirm Oct–Nov; Meta CPMs peak +30–50% in Nov. Buy early.
- **Albert Square is a Christmas Market hub** (~7 Nov–22 Dec, unconfirmed) — six weeks of peak footfall at the door.
- Honest constraint stated to the client: £200/mo cannot exit Meta's learning phase on booking conversions. Framed as awareness + demand capture (~30k impressions/month). Never promise a booking count.

## Online presence audit — verified 13 Aug 2026
Strengths: TripAdvisor 4.3/5 (748 reviews, #144 of 2,824); Quandoo 5.5/6 (117); press in Manchester's Finest, Manchester Confidential ×2, Good Food Guide; no other Armenian/Georgian restaurant in the city centre.

Problems (on the old site / listings):
- No booking button — phone only. NeroBooking widget not live.
- Menu served as a 2021 PDF.
- Opening hours wrong: "12am–11pm". Correct hours are on TripAdvisor.
- Address typo: "Albert Sqaure, 3-5 Princess Street".
- Not ranking for "restaurants near Albert Square" — aggregators own those terms.
- Three Instagram handles. Name in four variants across listings. Duplicate Deliveroo listings.
- No social links on site, no seasonal content, no embedded map.
- Absent from OpenTable, SquareMeal, DesignMyNight, Time Out.
- Google rating/review count could NOT be verified externally — never quote a figure without checking.

Competitor note: Maray has SevenRooms booking, blog, gift cards, large-groups page. Habas rebranded to theblackcatclub.co.uk — recheck before citing.
