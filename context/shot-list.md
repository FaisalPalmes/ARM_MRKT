# Armenian Taverna — shot list

*Living document. Shoot days: Sat 5, Sun 6, Sat 12, Sun 13 September 2026.*
*Kit: Sony A6700 · Sigma 18-50 f/2.8 · 16-50 kit · DJI gimbal · Osmo Nano · action cam*
*Last updated 19 August 2026.*

---

## HOW TO USE THIS

Read on your phone in the restaurant. Tick as you go.

**Priority key**
- 🔴 **BANNER** — a specific website section fails without it. Get these first, get them twice.
- 🟠 **CORE** — the site or the ads are noticeably weaker without it.
- ⚪ **BANK** — content library. Take as many as time allows.

**Three rules that apply to every 🔴 shot**

1. **Two crops, composed separately.** A 21:9 desktop frame and a 4:5 phone frame. Not one cropped down afterwards — recompose and shoot again. The subject sits in a different place in each.
2. **Leave a hole for the words.** Every banner shot needs one quadrant that is visually calm. It is specified per shot below. A beautiful frame with no empty space cannot be a hero.
3. **Shoot it on two different days.** Light changes, service changes, you get better. The four-day spread exists for this.

## 1B-2b. THE TRIPTYCH — three frames that must work as a set

For the ceremonial banquet section. These are shot **together**, same light, same session, or the symmetry falls apart.

- [ ] 🟠 **Centre panel — the tallest and strongest.** A full banquet table shot straight on, symmetrical, everything down at once. 3:4.35 portrait.
- [ ] 🟠 **Left panel** — the vegetarian spread, or a meze close-up. 3:4 portrait.
- [ ] 🟠 **Right panel** — the mixed khorovats platter. 3:4 portrait.

**Rules:** identical lighting and white balance across all three, shot from the same distance and height. The centre frame must be the best of the three — on a phone the two side panels are dropped and only the centre survives.

## 1B-2c. THE TILED-REVEAL FRAME — one shot, high stakes

A single wide frame for the homepage bar section, sliced into nine on the page.

- [ ] 🔴 **The bar at work.** Wide, 16:10. Dark, dense and detailed — bottles, glassware, hands, warm light, deep shadow.

**Why it matters:** every ninth of this frame has to be interesting on its own. A bright or empty image tiles into nine boring squares. Think of it as nine photographs that happen to line up — subject matter spread across the whole frame, no dead corners.

Shoot several. It's one frame, so it's cheap, but it carries a whole section.

# SECTION 2 — VIDEO

## 2A. WEBSITE VIDEO

- [ ] 🔴 **Hero background loop.** 8–12 seconds, silent, seamless, almost no motion — steam rising, coals breathing, a slow drift across the room. It must loop invisibly. Shoot 30 seconds and cut the calmest 10.
- [ ] 🟠 **Gimbal walk-through.** Front door → through the room → past the bar → resting on a laid table. One take, slow, no cuts. This single clip does more for a restaurant site than any other video.
- [ ] 🟠 Slow push in on the grill, skewers turning

## 2B. THE MONEY SHOTS — ads and reels

These are what Meta ads will run on. Vertical 9:16, shot vertical, not cropped.

- [ ] 🔴 **Skewer turn, slow motion.** 120fps, coals flaring. The most repostable three seconds you will get.
- [ ] 🔴 **Lavash slapped into the oven wall and pulled out puffed.** Nobody in Manchester has this footage.
- [ ] 🔴 **The pour.** Wine into a glass, backlit, slow motion.
- [ ] 🟠 **Meze landing.** Plates arriving on a table one after another, sped up slightly.
- [ ] 🟠 **The cut.** Knife through lamb, juice, steam.
- [ ] 🟠 Pomegranate seeds scattered over a dish in slow motion
- [ ] 🟠 Flames catching when fat drips
- [ ] ⚪ Cheese pull from khachapuri
- [ ] ⚪ Sour cream swirling into borsch
- [ ] ⚪ Steam escaping a khinkali on the first bite

## 2C. SEQUENCES — 15–30 second reels

Each is three to five clips that cut together into a finished post.

- [ ] 🟠 **One dish, start to finish.** Raw → marinated → on the skewer → over coals → plated → first bite. The highest-performing food format there is.
- [ ] 🟠 **Lavash, start to finish.** Dough → rolled → into the oven → out puffed → torn at the table.
- [ ] 🟠 **Arriving.** Street → door opens → room → seated → menu → first plate down. The "come here tonight" ad.
- [ ] ⚪ **The bar.** A drink being built end to end. *(Sophie, if she's comfortable on camera.)*
- [ ] ⚪ **Banquet reveal.** Empty table → plates arriving in fast motion → full table.

## 2D. AUDIO — do not forget this

Reels with real sound massively outperform reels with music over silence.

- [ ] Sizzle and fat spitting on the grill, close
- [ ] Room tone during service — chatter, cutlery
- [ ] Wine pouring
- [ ] Lavash tearing
- [ ] The oven door
- [ ] Glasses set down on a table

Record 30 seconds of each with the Osmo Nano close in. Label the files on the day.

## 2E. INTERVIEW / SPEAKING — optional, high value

If Arman is willing, on Day 3 or 4 once he is relaxed with the camera:

# SECTION 4 — TECHNICAL

- **Format:** RAW for stills. Video 4K, and 120fps for anything intended as slow motion.
- **Crops:** every 🔴 shot gets a 21:9 and a 4:5 version, composed separately.
- **Vertical:** shoot social vertically at the time. Cropping a horizontal frame to 9:16 always looks like a crop.
- **White balance:** the room is warm tungsten, the windows are cool daylight. Set it manually per area and stay consistent, or the grade will fight you.
- **Batteries:** two spares plus the dual charger — still flagged as the only item with genuine delivery risk. Order them.
- **Cards:** offload and back up at the end of every single day. Four days of footage on one card is a single point of failure.
- **Balance:** the cage and the Sigma both change the gimbal balance. Rebalance before Day 1, not on Day 1.
