# Content pillars and calendar anchors

## The six pillars

Named in the August strategy and in the client-facing plan. Keep the names.

| Pillar | What it is | Rough share |
|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Khorovats. Skewers, coals, hands. The differentiator. | 25% |
| **Table** | One dish, its story, its origin. Basturma, lavash, pasus dolma, khinkali. Meze all at once. | 20% |
| **58 Years** | Heritage. Three families. The diaspora menu. | 15% |
| **Cellar** | Armenian brandy, Georgian wine, the bar and lounge. The half of the brand nobody tells. | 15% |
| **Calendar** | Early Bird (the only place a price appears), banquets, seasonal menus, dated moments. | 15% |
| **Room** | The space, occasions, groups, private hire. | 10% |

Every post carries exactly one pillar in its frontmatter. Monthly calendars state the mix.

Frontmatter keys: `fire` · `table` · `58-years` · `cellar` · `calendar` · `room`. Asset tags use the same keys: `pillar:fire`.

## Channels

**Instagram + Facebook organic.** Same idea, adapted per platform — not cross-posted verbatim. Instagram leads on image and reels; Facebook leads on the older local audience and events.

**Meta ads — the £390 line.** £200/month budget. Objective: enquiries and awareness, not bookings — the budget can't exit learning phase on conversions. Landing: NeroBooking widget or the Early Bird page. Two creatives live at all times, one always being tested. Cellar content needs age targeting.

**Email — the £60 line.** Brevo, Arman's account. **One per week.** Double opt-in with the 10% welcome code. Unsubscribe on every send. Formats: *this week at the grill* · *one dish, one story* · *the bar on Thursday* · *the offer* · occasionally *a note from Arman* (ghost-written, approved by him personally).

## Dates that drive the calendar

| Date | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **21 Sept** — Armenian Independence Day | Content moment every year |
| **~7 Nov – 22 Dec** — Albert Square Christmas Market *(dates unconfirmed)* | Six weeks of peak footfall at the door |
| **December** | Biggest revenue window. **December is sold in October** — festive bookings confirm Oct–Nov, and Meta CPMs rise 30–50% in November. Buy early. |
| **6 Jan** — Armenian Christmas | Content moment |
| **Valentine's, Mother's Day, Father's Day** | Standard UK moments |
| **24 Apr** — Genocide Remembrance Day | **Commemorative only. Never commercial. No offers, no CTA, no ad spend within a week of it.** |
| **Mid-July** — Vardavar (movable) | Unique. No Manchester competitor can copy it. Content at minimum; an event if Arman has appetite. |
| **15 Nov – 1 Jan** | Early Bird unavailable — don't promote it |
