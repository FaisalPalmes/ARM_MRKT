# Content pillars and channels

Source: `context/handover.md` §6.

## Pillars

| Key | Pillar | What it is | Rough share |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grill` | **The grill** | Khorovats. Fire, skewers, coals, hands. The differentiator. | 25% |
| `dishes` | **The dishes** | One dish, its story, its origin. Basturma, lavash, pasus dolma, khinkali. | 20% |
| `story` | **1968 and the story** | Heritage. Three families. The diaspora menu. The room. | 15% |
| `bar` | **The bar and lounge** | Armenian brandy, Georgian wine, cocktails. The half of the brand nobody tells. | 15% |
| `offers` | **Offers** | Early Bird (the only place a price appears). Banquets. Seasonal menus. | 15% |
| `occasions` | **Occasions** | Groups, birthdays, private hire, Christmas. | 10% |

Use the `key` in content frontmatter (`pillar: grill`). State the month's mix at the top of each calendar file and compare it against these shares.

## Channels

**Instagram + Facebook organic.** Same content, adapted per platform — not cross-posted verbatim. Instagram leads on image and reels; Facebook leads on the older local audience and events. Sophie currently posts; the plan is Faisal produces, Sophie or Faisal posts, always after approval. Channel keys: `instagram`, `facebook`.

**Meta ads — the £390 line.** £200/month budget. Objective: enquiries and bookings, not reach. Landing: the NeroBooking widget or the Early Bird page. Two creatives live at all times, one always being tested. Alcohol content needs age targeting per Meta policy. Channel key: `meta-ads`.

**Email — the £60 line.** Brevo. One per week. Double opt-in with the 10% welcome code. GDPR: consent wording, privacy policy on the site, unsubscribe on every send. Channel key: `email`.

Email formats that fit a weekly rhythm:
- *this week at the grill*
- *one dish, one story*
- *the bar on Thursday*
- *the offer*
- occasionally *a note from Arman* — ghost-written in his voice, approved by him personally.

**Google Business Profile.** Weekly post cadence once manager access exists (posts live 7 days). Plan in `context/google-profile-plan.md` once imported.
