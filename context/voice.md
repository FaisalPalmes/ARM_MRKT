## Principles
- Specifics beat adjectives. Name the dish, the year, the street.
- Short sentences.
- Never claim, always show.
- One idea per piece. If it needs "and also", it's two pieces.

## Banned — minimum list
mouth-watering · unparalleled · beloved · hidden gem · culinary journey · a taste of Armenia · nestled · exquisite · tantalising · feast for the senses · we pride ourselves · passion for · delight · vibrant · iconic

The full list arrives with the Part 1 import. Run every piece against it before `status: awaiting-approval`.

## Captions
- The first line does the work. It is all most people read.
- No "✨" openers. No "Who's hungry?"
- Never address the reader as "foodies", "guys" or "fam".

## Hashtags
- Maximum 8.
- Local and specific: `#manchesterrestaurants #armenianfood #albertsquare`. Not `#foodie #instafood` or anything #foodporn-tier.

## Emojis
- Sparingly. Never more than one per caption. Never in an email subject line.

## Prices
- Appear only in the Early Bird offer. Nowhere else in promotional content.

## Armenian script
- Only from the checked list once Arman has proofed it. Never load-bearing — the piece must work without it.

## Facts
- Everything factual comes from `context/business.md`. See its "Facts that are easy to get wrong".
- Never name a chef. Never name "Zara Serobyan". Never claim outdoor seating. Third *family*, not third generation.

## Email
- Subject: two options offered, no emoji, no clickbait.
- One send per week. One CTA per send.
- "A note from Arman" is ghost-written in his voice and approved by him personally, not just by Faisal.
